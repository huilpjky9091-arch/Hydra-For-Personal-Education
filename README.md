# HYDRA — THE IMMORTAL FRESHWATER POLYP

Interactive 3D Digital Biology Museum & Laboratory exploring the anatomy, cell biology, nematocyst discharge biophysics, digestion, enzyme secretion, diffusion excretion, physiology, regeneration, and reproduction of *Hydra* (Phylum Cnidaria, Class Hydrozoa).

## Features

- **3D Interactive Hero Specimen**: Orbit, zoom, pan 360°, and toggle **X-Ray Mode** to inspect translucent layers and internal cavities.
- **Interactive 3D Anatomy Explorer (12 Structures)**: Clickable 3D parts (`Hypostome`, `Mouth`, `Tentacles`, `Cnidocytes`, `Nematocysts`, `Epidermis`, `Gastrodermis`, `Mesoglea`, `Gastrovascular Cavity`, `Bud`, `Body Column`, `Basal Disc`) with **Anatomy Cutaway Mode**, 3D labels, and enlarged structural cross-sections.
- **3D Microscopic Explorer (8 Cell Types)**: Interactive `100x / 400x / 1000x` magnification and Fluorescence vs Natural optical stains for all 8 Hydra cell types.
- **Nematocyst 3D Discharge Simulator**: 6-step slow-motion firing simulation covering all 5 components (`Cnidocyte`, `Nematocyst Capsule`, `Coiled Tubule`, `Operculum`, `Cnidocil`) and 4 distinct mechanisms (`Stenotele`, `Desmoneme`, `Holotrichous Isorhiza`, `Atrichous Isorhiza`).
- **Two-Stage Digestive System & Enzyme Secretion Labs**: Interactive extracellular vs. intracellular digestion simulator (including single-mouth egestion) and variable-speed Gland Cell enzyme secretion diagram.
- **Excretion Diffusion Simulator**: Visualizes ammonotelic nitrogenous waste ($\text{NH}_3$) and respiratory gas ($\text{O}_2/\text{CO}_2$) diffusion across body surfaces without specialized excretory organs.
- **3D Interactive Feeding Laboratory**: Spawn *Daphnia* prey, trigger tentacle contact & nematocyst firing, watch mouth dilation, extracellular/intracellular digestion, and egestion.
- **Physiology, 3D Regeneration Timeline & 3D Reproduction**: Integrated 7-system cell-tissue map, 0–72h regeneration timeline (3 independent stem cell lineages + morphallaxis), and Asexual Budding vs. Sexual Embryotheca reproduction.
- **Knowledge Center & 33-Question Quiz**: 11 comprehensive university-intro biology topics with per-topic interactive quizzes and scientific references.
